Every day, perfectly good food and stuff gets thrown away. Fresh bread. A warm jacket. A stack of books.
Meanwhile, a neighbour just two streets away could really use them. They simply never find each other.
Meet EcoShare. Share food and things with your neighbours.
Start with a photo. Free, open AI models fill in the title, category and allergens for you. You check every field before it goes live.
Explore shows what's near you. The For you feed ranks items by your interests, distance and freshness.
See something you like? One tap requests it. Agree a pickup time in chat, while AI screens messages for abuse.
At pickup, a one-time code confirms the handover. Credits only move once the exchange is done. No code, no payment.
And every share counts. See the carbon you've helped save, earn badges, and watch your EcoTree grow.
